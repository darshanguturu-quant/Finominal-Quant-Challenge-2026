E. Annualized Sharpe Ratio, Cumulative Return, and CAGR

  • Annualized Sharpe Ratio: $$\text{SR} = \left(\frac{\mu_{p, daily}}{\sigma_{p, daily}}\right) \times \sqrt{252}$$ where $\mu = \frac{1}{N}\sum R_t$, $\sigma$ uses sample standard deviation with $\text{ddof}=1$ ($s = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N-1}\sum (R_t - \mu)^2}$), and risk-free rate is $0.0%$.
  • Cumulative Return: $$\text{CR} = \prod_{t=1}^N (1 + R_{p, t}) - 1$$
  • CAGR: $$\text{CAGR} = (1 + \text{CR})^{\frac{252}{N}} - 1$$
